## Verdict first

**Honesty is not the gate. Distribution and data are.** Dropping the honesty policy buys entry
into the snake-oil lane — the single most crowded lane (Hone + IObit + CCleaner + 20 clones),
where incumbents own the influencer pipelines and the only differentiator Compy has (trust/receipts)
gets torched on entry. The dirty lane fails the owner's own constraint AND the math. This is not
ethics; it's ROI: a no-budget entrant cannot out-Hone Hone.

**The actual problem:** honesty has been deployed as a vow of silence instead of a weapon. Compy
refuses to brag because it refuses to collect the receipts that would make bragging truthful.

## The transformation (what Compy becomes)

From: "an optimizer that promises nothing."
To: **"the measurement brand that proves everything — and gets paid when the proof says 'buy a GPU.'"**

Free app forever (= trust engine + funnel). Money comes from around it:

### The policy rewrite — Receipts Doctrine

DELETE (these were self-gags, not virtues):
- **No-telemetry absolutism** → opt-in anonymous results sharing ("share your measured results to
  power community stats"). This is THE unlock: aggregate real data → marketing claims that are
  exciting AND true ("RX 6600 rigs measured +X% 1%-lows after these 3 fixes — N=412"). Hone claims
  with vibes; Compy can claim with data.
- **"Your PC is probably fine" defensive copy** → "Measured on rigs like yours." Celebrate measured
  wins loudly: share cards, before/afters, aggregate stats, leaderboard-style receipts.
- **Refusal to market** → attack marketing. Pick the fight: "$X/mo optimizers, measured." The debunk
  IS the ad. Drama sells; receipts make it safe.

KEEP (load-bearing, not moral):
- **No fabricated claims** (legal exposure + one Reddit thread = brand death; this is the owner's
  integrity line and it's also just survival).
- **Rollback-first** (the moat; B2B's whole purchase reason).
- **Real measurement** (it IS the product).
- Frozen risky-tweak catalog (liability), advisory features keep growing (already the plan).

REJECTED — dark patterns (fake scan counts, fear popups, fake scarcity, hard-cancel subs):
crowded lane, torched differentiator, permanent brand damage in gaming communities. Fails owner
constraint + fails capitalism. Not a morals call.
