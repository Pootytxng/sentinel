*2026-06-06, Cowork, owner-blessed. The owner's framing: the surface app serves everyone (foam
pit — nothing must ever be learned); beneath it, a quiet layer rewards the users who STUDY

## The Six Laws of the Shadow

1. **The surface never pays for the shadow.** Nothing hidden may be the only path to any value
   (foam-pit law is supreme). Delete every egg and Compy loses zero function — eggs are pure
   bonus craftsmanship.
2. **Never lie, even in play.** No fake numbers, no joke metrics, nothing that could be
   mistaken for real measurement. Eggs live in copy, visuals, and micro-interactions — never
   in data.
3. **No economy.** No unlocks, no levels, no progression, no collection UI, no "3 of 12 eggs
   found." Discovery is its own whole reward — a wink, not a trophy case. (Decision A — the
   de-gamification — is NOT reversed by this doctrine; the paused cosmetic-reward direction
   stays a separate, paused conversation.)
4. **Quiet by design.** Eggs never interrupt, notify, popup, or animate uninvited. They wait
   to be found. Step-aside courtesy applies in full (game running = shadow sleeps too).
5. **Sacred surfaces carry no eggs:** exports/receipts, the Trust Ledger's data, error states,
   consent/approval moments, anything measurement-adjacent. (A receipt with a joke in it is a
   receipt you can't show anyone.)
6. **In THE VOICE.** When an egg speaks, it speaks Compy's familiar, warm, plain-English
   humor — never memes-of-the-month, never cringe, never punching at the user.

1. **The Konami code** (the canonical gamer handshake), entered anywhere in the app: the
   wordmark's pulse re-draws itself once (the launch heartbeat replay) + one quiet line near
   it: *"You remembered. Compy remembers too."* Fades on its own. Nothing changes.
2. **Fidgeter's reward:** triple-click the pulse-C wordmark → the heartbeat draw-animation
   replays. (Fidget-discovery is the most natural shadow path there is.)

   finds a real entry — a short, in-voice piece on cheat codes' history ending: *"Compy has
   no cheats. Well. Almost."* (It's a true Library entry — verdict-style honesty about WHY
   real 'FPS cheat codes' don't exist — so even the egg teaches.)
4. **The patient gaze:** hovering the posture ring uninterrupted for ~10s while everything is
   Dialed → the ring takes one slow extra breath and the subline swaps once to *"Nothing to
   fix. Go play."* then back. (Respects reduced-motion: text swap only.)
5. **Hearth keeper:** with the Hearth theme active during December, the ambient warmth drifts
   ~2% warmer. No UI, no mention, ever. (Pure craftsmanship tier — the egg nobody may ever
   consciously notice.)

## Sequencing + constraints

- **Idle-filler tier only:** eggs ride while HQ is parked or alongside unrelated trains —
  they NEVER preempt imperatives, never block a wave, never appear in release notes as
  features. Standing-priority governance applies.
- Each egg: frontend-only, tokens-only, zero IPC, individually revertable, reduced-motion
  honored, lint/test discipline same as everything (an egg with a bug is a bug).
- Tester reports mentioning an egg = pure signal of the studious (note it in the tracker);
  never marketed, never hinted at in docs. The README/landing page stay silent forever.

## Acceptance (per egg)

- [ ] Found only by deliberate attention; invisible to every normal path.
- [ ] All Six Laws hold (checklist each against 1–6).
- [ ] Removing the egg leaves zero trace in behavior, data, or layout.
