## The convergence finding (context for synthesis, not fed to personas)

Through three rounds, four of five personas have independently converged on a similar guessed
target — not a total beginner, not an enthusiast, but a gamer already wary of fake "optimizer"
tools who wants proof over hype (the "recovering skeptic"). The Casual Owner — the persona actually
built to match Compy's own stated internal target — is the one whose guess has moved the most, and
as of 2026-07-14 she now sees Expert View as deliberate evidence the product is being built to
serve both her and the power-user crowd without conflict. That shift, sustained across two
follow-up rounds now, is the strongest longitudinal signal this panel has produced.
