What the review caught before you saw it

My first attempt slid a bright strip across the bar and hid the overflow so it couldn’t escape. But the bar is also the box the number sits in — so on a very short bar the hiding cut the number in half. The review found 999 rendering as 99. The shipped version moves the light through the bar’s background instead: nothing ever leaves the box, so nothing needs hiding.

Reduced motion removes it completely — absent, not frozen mid-glow. If your system is set to reduce motion, the bars above are already still and that is the correct behaviour. Four other things in Compy move light: the title’s arrival shine, the “checking…” scan line, the setup-tour loading placeholder, and a dial filling up. I ruled none of them a celebration — they say arriving, working, loading and progress, not you won. Say the word if you read any of them as stealing the moment and I’ll take it out.
