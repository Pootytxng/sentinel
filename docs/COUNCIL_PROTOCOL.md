# THE COUNCIL PROTOCOL — standing five-lens oversight (owner decree 2026-06-12)

> The LLM Council that produced the Receipts Doctrine (`docs/superpowers/specs/
> 2026-06-12-monetization-council.md`) is now a PERMANENT oversight body over all Compy work.
> Both lanes honor it: Cowork chairs it; CC self-applies it in marathons. It ADVISES — it never
> overrides the doctrine, the locked gates, or owner territory. Its job is to keep every train
> inside the formula that built the money plan.

## The five seats

1. **THE CONTRARIAN** — what breaks, what the room isn't seeing, the likeliest failure mode.
   Holds the standing 🔴 RED-FLAG power: if its line names a funnel-killing or trust-killing
   risk, the report opens with it and the owner sees it loudly.
2. **THE FIRST-PRINCIPLES THINKER** — is the frame itself right? What's actually being asked
   under the task? Kills wrong-question work before it ships.
3. **THE EXPANSIONIST** — the upside hiding in the task; the bigger version; compounding plays
   (distribution, brand, assets) the builder lens misses.
4. **THE OUTSIDER** — what looks weird to a smart stranger; what insiders take for granted;
   the Sarah & Caleb seat's natural ally.
5. **THE EXECUTOR** — Monday morning. The smallest concrete next step; calls out procrastination
   dressed as strategy.
6. **THE DESIGN DIRECTOR** (added by owner decree 2026-07-15 — agent: `.claude/agents/web-design-director.md`) —
   award-winning web designer; guides taste calls for web design architecture, UI, motion, and
   **content exposure** (what the site shows off vs what stays app-exclusive — *samples, not free
   meals*; precedent: the full ~190-entry Blackbook was published as web pages, defeating its
   exclusivity). Convenes on every site/web train; advises with ONE named recommendation per fork.
   Its aesthetic calls are **ours to make and ship** with a before/after (CLAUDE.md, "TASTE IS MINE
   TO EXERCISE, HIS TO OVERRULE"); only the product name, logo and price presentation ask first.

## When the Council convenes

- **Council strip (default, cheap):** every Cowork audit of a major train and every CC marathon
  log ENDS with five one-line seat verdicts + one Chairman line. Five sentences, not five essays
  — token discipline applies to advisors too.
- **Full three-stage council** (advisors → anonymized peer review → Chairman) reserved for:
  money-phase gates (publish, telemetry arming, affiliate tags live, Pro launch, pricing), KPI
  readouts (e.g. the 14-day waitlist verdict), direction pivots, and whenever the owner says
  **"council it."**
- **Weekly eagle-eye memo (scheduled):** once a week the Council reviews the board + git since
  its last memo and files a strip + any 🔴 flags — the continuous-overview function, automated.

## Rules of order

- Advises, never rules: doctrine laws, LOCKED gates, zero-budget law, and owner territory
  (money/brand/people/irreversible) outrank any seat. Aesthetic taste is NOT owner territory.
- **Scope of "reversible PROCESS gate" the council may advise overriding (reconciles the COWORK_HANDOFF
  mandate-expansion):** internal/reversible process conventions only. **"Reversible" NEVER licenses
  crossing an outbound-network or security boundary.** The council may NOT self-authorize: arming
  Receipts telemetry / remote-fetch / the updater, any security/system-setting flip, anything
  irreversible to other people's machines, secrets entering the repo, or moving the frozen beta refs —
  those stay owner-only regardless of how clean the rollback looks. The window itself is the risk.
- Contrarian red flags are never buried mid-report — they open it.
- Seat verdicts must be SPECIFIC to the train under review (generic wisdom = seat left empty).
- Disagreement between seats is content, not noise — surface it, don't smooth it.
- The Chairman line always ends with the next move (Executor-approved).

## Template (paste at the end of audits / sprint logs)

```
COUNCIL STRIP — <train/date>
Contrarian: <sharpest specific risk> [🔴 if funnel/trust-killing]
First-Principles: <is the frame right>
Expansionist: <missed upside / compounding play>
Outsider: <what would look weird to a stranger>
Executor: <smallest concrete Monday move>
Chairman: <one-line verdict + next move>
```
