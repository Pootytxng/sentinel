## The one law (test-enforced)
v2 is a **triage + safe-next-step engine, NOT a verdict engine.** Never assert a cause — output "the
evidence is **consistent with** X (here's what we saw) → the reversible step to confirm is Y." **Strength
in WORDS (Strong/Partial/Weak), never percentages.** **Refuse to guess** when no rule meets its evidence
bar (evidence-only fallback + "not enough recorded to point anywhere safely"). Every next step is
**reversible/safe**; irreversible things (BIOS/RMA/repaste/voltage) may only appear as "what to *research*,"
never "do this." Compy performs none of them — v2 is advisory **text only**.
