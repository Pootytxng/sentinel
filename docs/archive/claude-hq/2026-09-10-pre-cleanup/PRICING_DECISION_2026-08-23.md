## ⚖️ AUTO-RENEWAL LAW — a cost the subscription model carries alone

The FTC's "click-to-cancel" Negative Option Rule was **vacated by the Eighth Circuit in July 2025**;
the FTC restarted rulemaking with an ANPRM to OIRA on **2026-01-30**, comment through **2026-04-13**.
**But ROSCA remains fully in force** — clear pre-billing disclosure, express informed consent, simple
cancellation, with recent FTC settlements of $7.5M and $60M. State ARLs (CA, CO, MN, NY) are
expanding; a California settlement in Aug 2025 was $7.5M and a Nov 2025 multi-state one $4.8M.

**Plain terms: a subscription obliges a solo developer to build compliant disclosure, a self-serve
cancel flow, renewal reminders and dunning — and to track a moving federal rule plus 50 state
regimes, permanently, alone, for a $4.99 line. A one-time sale carries none of this.**

## 🕳️ DOES THE LIFETIME TRAP APPLY? NO

The standard argument is that lifetime pricing collapses when each customer carries ongoing marginal
cost. **Compy's recurring cost per customer is approximately zero** — no telemetry backend, no
server, no accounts, a static installer download. Recurring costs are *per-product, not per-customer*:
signing (~$9.99/mo), a domain, and the owner's time. **The classic argument does not bite here.** It
bites only on support labour (cap it with a written scope) and on LTV ceiling (a luxury problem with
zero customers).

## 🔒 THE CONSTRAINT EVERY OTHER PLAN IGNORES — NO TELEMETRY

Compy ships a public promise of no telemetry, **so conversion, funnel, churn and usage cannot be
measured.** Any model that requires measurement to operate is **not operable here.** Most pricing
advice on the internet assumes the opposite. **A model that must be tuned by metrics is disqualified
by the product's own promise** — and that promise is a selling point worth more than the tuning.

**Upgrade triggers must therefore be local and in-app**, e.g. *"you've applied this profile 4 times
by hand; Pro switches it automatically."* Nothing leaves the machine. **You will never know your
conversion rate. That is the price of the promise.**

## 🆓 FREE vs PAID — and this is compliance, not generosity

**Free forever, never gated:** hardware detection · the plain-English explanation of every proposed
change · applying changes · the snapshot · **and rollback.** **Rollback is the promise. Gating it
turns "shows its receipts" into a hostage situation.**

**Paid ($29):** saved profiles and per-game switching · scheduled/automatic re-apply · one-click bulk
apply · receipt export and full history · the advanced tweak set. **All convenience and breadth,
never safety.**

**This is not optional.** Microsoft's criteria name software that *"display[s] claims in an alarming
manner about your device's health and require[s] payment… in exchange for fixing the purported
problems."* A free tier that scores your PC scarily and paywalls the fix is the textbook trigger.
**A free tier that actually fixes and reverts, and sells convenience, is not.**
