# Compy Private Beta — Tester Tracker (owner's private local file)

> **Copy this to `BETA_TESTER_TRACKER.local.md` and fill that in** — the `.local` copy is gitignored,
> so real tester handles/notes never enter git. Compy's privacy ethos applies to Compy's own beta.

**How to use:** one row per tester; fill cells as they progress (a date or ✅). **Not shipped in the
app, no telemetry** — this is just your local scoreboard so ~20 invites don't fall through the
cracks. The two things that matter most per tester are the **export receipt** and the
**one-sentence pitch** (that sentence is the real product test).

**⭐ Flag NVIDIA testers — they're extra valuable:** NVIDIA GPU sensing is implemented but *not yet
hardware-verified*, so an NVIDIA tester doubles as the hardware validation Compy can't do on the
AMD dev box. Prioritize recruiting + chasing them.

## 📋 Paste when a tester goes quiet

> Hey! No pressure at all — when you next get a gaming session in with Compy running, two tiny
> things would help me a ton:
> 1. Hit **Export** after you play and send me the file (it's a local report — you see it first,
>    nothing leaves your PC until you send it).
> 2. One sentence: **how would you describe Compy to a friend?** Even "honestly, it's just X" is
>    gold — that sentence tells me whether the whole idea lands.
>
> And if any part felt dumb or confusing, that's the most useful thing you can tell me.

*Why these two: the export receipt is the only "telemetry" a local-first beta has (voluntary,
honest), and the one-sentence pitch is the real test of whether the product explains itself.*
