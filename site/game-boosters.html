Game booster apps (Razer Cortex etc.)

Also searched as: game booster · razer cortex · wise game booster · smart game booster · booster app

What it does

All-in-one 'booster' apps that promise FPS by closing background apps and 'optimizing' RAM or CPU when a game launches.

The honest verdict

The one real thing they do — closing background apps — is free to do yourself, and Windows already deprioritizes background work while a game has focus. The 'RAM optimization' half is the standby-purge placebo, and the booster itself stays resident, sometimes with its own overlay on top. On a healthy rig there's nothing measurable left for it to add.

How Compy treats it

Compy won't recommend it. If you're curious anyway: change one thing at a time, measure it on your own rig, and revert unless it's clearly better — that loop is exactly what Compy is built around.
