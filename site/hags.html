Hardware-Accelerated GPU Scheduling (HAGS)

Also searched as: hags · gpu scheduling · hardware accelerated gpu scheduling

What it does

Moves GPU scheduling from the OS to the GPU itself (reboot required).

The honest verdict

Helps latency and is required for some frame-gen — but occasionally regresses specific titles. Worth testing on, with a measured before/after rather than vibes.
