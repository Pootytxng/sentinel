High / Ultimate Performance power plan

Also searched as: power plan · ultimate performance · balanced vs high performance

What it does

Stops Windows from downclocking/parking CPU cores at light load.

The honest verdict

Real but narrower than it sounds: it mainly steadies frame pacing in CPU-bound games (and stops light-load core downclocking), with little to gain in GPU-bound ones — at the cost of idle watts. Compy can switch plans for you, with the exact previous plan recorded for rollback.

How Compy treats it

Where it genuinely fits a rig, Compy can recommend it — applied with a snapshot first and a one-click rollback after, like every change it makes.

The Compy Blackbook holds 191 graded entries like this one, judged against your hardware — and every change it does make is reversible, with the receipt to prove it.
