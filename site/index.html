Every optimizer promises speed. Most deliver placebo — some break things. Compy reads your actual hardware, grades the internet's favorite tweaks honestly — real, placebo, or risky — and measures what actually changed on your rig. When a fix is real, you see the numbers. When it isn't, you see that too. And everything it changes, it can undo: exact prior state, kept in a receipt on your PC.

Real

It actually does something. A measurable or well-proven effect on your games — worth doing.

Placebo

All hype, no effect. Popular online, but it changes nothing you can measure. We skip these so you don't waste time chasing them.

Risky — refused

Not worth the downside. It might do something, but it brings real tradeoffs — instability, a security hole, or things breaking — so Compy refuses it, or lets you decide with eyes open.
