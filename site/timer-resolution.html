Forcing 0.5 ms global timer resolution

Also searched as: timer resolution · 0.5ms timer · timerresolution.exe

What it does

Forces Windows' interrupt timer to its finest interval system-wide.

The honest verdict

This one does change things you can measure — just not reliably for the better, which is why it isn't a free win. A minority of systems get slightly steadier lows, some get worse, many see no change, and it raises idle power draw the whole time the timer is pinned; on a few specific older games it can also lift an odd frame-rate ceiling. Because the effect is inconsistent and comes with that cost, treat it as a measure-and-revert experiment, not a set-and-forget tweak: change it alone, watch your own numbers, and put it back unless it is clearly better on your machine.

How Compy treats it

It's on Compy's refuse list. Compy will never apply this tweak — and if a guide already talked you into it, reversibility is the whole point of the app.
