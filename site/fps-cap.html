FPS cap below refresh rate

Also searched as: frame cap · max frame rate · limit fps · 141 cap

What it does

Caps frames ~3 below your monitor's refresh (e.g. 141 on a 144 Hz panel).

The honest verdict

Keeps VRR engaged, avoids V-Sync backpressure, stabilizes frametimes, and lowers heat. One of the few tweaks that's real on nearly every setup.
