Upscaling (DLSS / FSR / XeSS quality modes)

Also searched as: dlss · fsr · xess · upscaling

What it does

Renders at lower resolution and AI-upscales to native.

The honest verdict

The rare free lunch: quality modes usually give MORE real FPS and LOWER latency than native with near-native image quality. Distinct from frame-gen (which adds latency).
